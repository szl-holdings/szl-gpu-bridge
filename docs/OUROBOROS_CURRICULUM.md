# The Ouroboros Curriculum — 54 hours, then work, then learn, forever

**Directive:** the fleet trains on ALL in-house datasets plus curated open-source
material for 54 hours. Then the models go to work. While they work, they keep
learning: every worked task becomes curriculum via the receipts chain, and the
chain feeds the next training cycle. The loop audits itself (Law 10).

### The schedule
- **Hours 0–24:** constitution + prime principle + lean invariants (the soul)
- **Hours 24–48:** code + formulas + papers + verticals (the skills)
- **Hours 48–54:** proving-ground drills + eval gates (the proof)
- Every leg: boot receipt first, checkpoint push to HF every 2h, receipt to
  the chain every publish. A leg without receipts inside the sweep window is
  stopped and restarted (runbook rule).

## Phase 3 — The continuous loop (forever)

```
work a task → emit a receipt → receipt lands in frontier-54-runs
     ↑                                           │
     │                                           ▼
next training cycle  ←  curation (receipts become curriculum)
```

- Every worked task produces a receipt; receipts with outcomes become SFT pairs
- The gardener curates weekly: keep the pairs that passed gates, drop the rest
- Retrain weekly on the accumulated corpus (LoRA deltas, cheap, on the A6000s)
- The honesty battery gates every promotion: a model that gets worse at
  abstention does not ship, no matter how good it gets at tasks

**This is the Ouroboros:** the estate's work becomes its curriculum, the
curriculum improves the workers, the workers' receipts prove the improvement —
and the proving ground keeps the whole thing honest.

## The vision statement (for the record)

An AI estate where the models are raised on the constitution, trained on the
estate's own mind, put to work with receipts behind every action, and improved
forever by their own honest output — measured, gated, and machine-checked at
every cycle. The 54 hours are the childhood. The loop is the life.
